## What's been built

### Obsidian vault (obsidian/)
The primary working brain. Open `obsidian/` as a vault in Obsidian. All notes use wikilinks, callouts, and YAML frontmatter. The MOC (`000-Insight-Clinic-MOC.md`) is the master index.

### Social media (obsidian/Marketing/)
- `Social-Calendar-MayJun2026.md` — 4-week posting plan (28 May – 24 Jun 2026) with all 12 captions ready to copy-paste, hashtags, Higgsfield asset references, and compliance checklists.
- `Higgsfield-Scripts.md` — 6 AI video prompts for Reels (paste directly into Higgsfield), plus production workflow.
- `Brand-Guide.md` — practitioners, services, palette, voice, compliance (ASA/MHRA/BAcC).

### Outreach (obsidian/Outreach/)
7 email sequences (GP, osteopath, chiropractor, physio, counsellor, Pilates, yoga) — each with 3 emails + referral guide + in-person scripts. Plus a CRM tracker and print-ready A4 referral card.

### Health talks (obsidian/Health-Talks/)
Full fatigue talk with 12 slides and word-for-word speaker notes. 4 additional talk outlines. Delivery guide with studio booking email, promo copy, pacing guide, conversion script, rotation schedule.

### Website (website/)
3 complete HTML homepage concepts (clinical, warm, bold) + 4 additional pages (services, about, new patients, FAQ). Open directly in a browser — no server needed.

## How to continue working here

Common tasks you might ask Claude to do:
- "Write July's social media calendar" — follow the same format as `Social-Calendar-MayJun2026.md`
- "Add a new outreach sequence for [partner type]" — follow the format in any `*-Email-Sequence.md`
- "Update the outreach tracker with [contact]" — edit `obsidian/Outreach/Outreach-Tracker.md`
- "Write a talk on [topic]" — follow the format in `Talk-Fatigue.md`
- "Update the website copy for [page]" — edit the relevant HTML file in `website/`
- "Write a caption for [post idea]" — follow the voice and compliance rules in `Brand-Guide.md`

## Compliance rules (always apply)
- No specific cure claims for named conditions (ASA + MHRA)
- Patient stories: anonymised + written consent on file
- BAcC code: acupuncture claims must align with published evidence summaries
- Dr Ravi's title: "Integrative Medicines Physician" — not "GP" unless GMC-registered
- Avoid "energy" in Instagram captions (algorithm flags it) — use "rhythm", "regulation", "restoration"
- All medical content must be visibly authored or reviewed by a named practitioner
