Your First Visit

What happens, step by step

We've designed every step to be as clear and low-friction as possible — because starting somewhere new can feel daunting.

1

Book your appointment (or start with a discovery call)

Use the booking form on this page or call us directly. If you're not sure which service is right for you, our free 15-minute discovery call lets you speak with a practitioner before committing to a full appointment.

Tip: If you have existing blood test results or GP letters, note that in your booking so we can review them in advance.
2

Complete your intake questionnaire

We'll send a detailed intake form by email once your booking is confirmed. It covers your symptoms, medical history, lifestyle, medications, and what you're hoping to achieve. Please complete it at least 48 hours before your appointment — it allows your practitioner to prepare properly and means your session is spent on the consultation, not form-filling.

Tip: Be as thorough as you can. Even symptoms that seem unrelated are often relevant — connections between them are frequently where we find the root cause.
3

Your first appointment — 60 to 90 minutes

Your practitioner will have reviewed your intake form before you arrive. The appointment is a proper conversation — your full history, a chance to ask questions, and a thorough clinical assessment. We don't rush to conclusions. By the end, you'll have a clear sense of what we think is driving your symptoms and what we'd like to investigate or treat.

Tip: Bring any previous blood test results, scan reports, or specialist letters. Even results from years ago can be useful context.
4

Testing (where indicated)

If functional testing is appropriate, we'll order it at or after your first appointment. Most tests involve a blood draw, urine collection, or stool sample done at home or at our clinic — we'll walk you through each one clearly. Results typically come back within 1–3 weeks depending on the panel.

Tip: Don't supplement with high-dose vitamins in the 5 days before any nutrient testing — it can skew results. We'll remind you of this in your pre-appointment notes.
5

Results review and treatment plan

Once results are in, we'll book a review appointment to go through them in full and build your treatment plan. This is a detailed, personalised document — not a generic protocol. It covers nutrition, supplementation, lifestyle recommendations, and any therapies we're recommending, along with the rationale for each.

Tip: Write down questions between your first appointment and your results review. Patients who come with specific questions get more from this session.
6

Ongoing care and review

We track your progress against measurable markers — not just how you feel subjectively. Follow-up appointments are typically every 4–6 weeks initially, spacing out as you improve. We adjust your plan as your results shift and your needs change.

Setting Expectations

What integrative care is — and isn't

We want you to arrive knowing exactly what kind of care we provide and what to expect from working with us.

✅ You can expect

  • ✓ A practitioner who reads your notes before you arrive
  • ✓ A first appointment of at least 60 minutes
  • ✓ Advanced functional testing beyond standard NHS panels
  • ✓ A treatment plan with clear rationale for each recommendation
  • ✓ Honest timelines — we won't promise results we can't deliver
  • ✓ Collaboration with your GP if appropriate
  • ✓ Written summaries you can share with other clinicians
  • ✓ Practitioners who refer out when something is outside their scope

❌ We are not

  • ✗ An emergency or urgent care service — for emergencies, call 999 or attend A&E
  • ✗ A replacement for NHS primary care — we complement it
  • ✗ A quick fix — root-cause medicine takes time and commitment
  • ✗ A supplement shop — we prescribe therapeutically, not commercially
  • ✗ Able to prescribe NHS medications (though our integrative GP can prescribe privately where appropriate)
  • ✗ Able to guarantee outcomes — we can only work with evidence and your honest engagement with the process
