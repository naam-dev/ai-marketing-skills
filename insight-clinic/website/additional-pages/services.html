Acupuncture

British Acupuncture Council (BAcC) Registered

The evidence base for acupuncture continues to grow. NICE guidelines recommend it for chronic primary pain, tension-type headaches, and migraines. Beyond these, our practitioners have significant clinical experience treating the conditions listed below.

Conditions we treat with acupuncture:

  • ✓ Chronic pain — back, neck, joint, headache, migraine

Nutritional Therapy

BANT Registered · CNHC Accredited

Our nutritional therapists are BANT-registered (British Association for Nutrition and Lifestyle Medicine) — the professional standard for nutritional therapy in the UK. They use advanced functional testing to identify the nutritional and metabolic roots of your symptoms, then build a personalised protocol — not a generic diet plan.

The difference between nutritional therapy and dietetics is scope: we work with the full functional picture, using tests rarely ordered in conventional settings to find what's actually driving your symptoms.

We commonly identify and treat:

  • ✓ Gut dysbiosis, SIBO, and intestinal permeability
  • ✓ IBS, bloating, food sensitivities, and intolerances
  • ✓ Nutrient deficiencies — magnesium, B12, iron, zinc, CoQ10
  • ✓ Blood sugar dysregulation and insulin resistance
  • ✓ Thyroid support (nutritional component)
  • ✓ Hormonal conditions — PCOS, endometriosis, perimenopause

Service 04

Osteopathy

General Osteopathic Council (GOsC) Registered

Osteopathy is a whole-body approach to musculoskeletal health — regulated by the General Osteopathic Council (GOsC) and recognised by NICE for the treatment of low back pain. Our osteopaths don't just treat the site of pain; they investigate the postural patterns, movement habits, and systemic factors that are maintaining it.

Within the integrative clinic setting, our osteopaths collaborate with nutritional therapists and acupuncturists on complex cases — so patients with chronic inflammatory pain get both structural and systemic treatment.

We treat:

  • ✓ Lower back and neck pain — acute and chronic
  • ✓ Joint pain — hip, knee, shoulder, wrist
  • ✓ Sciatica and nerve pain
  • ✓ Sports injuries and post-surgical rehabilitation
  • ✓ Headaches and jaw tension (TMJ)
  • ✓ Postural problems from desk work
  • ✓ Pregnancy-related musculoskeletal pain

Advanced Testing

The tests that change everything

Standard NHS blood panels are designed to detect disease. These tests detect dysfunction — the gap where most unexplained symptoms live.

Hormones

DUTCH Complete Hormone Test

Dried urine test mapping oestrogen metabolism pathways, progesterone, testosterone, DHEA, and 24-hour cortisol rhythm. The gold standard for hormonal investigation — far beyond a single blood draw.

Gut Health

Comprehensive Stool Analysis

Microbiome mapping: bacterial diversity, pathogen presence, inflammatory markers, intestinal permeability (zonulin), digestive enzyme function, and short-chain fatty acid production.

Gut Health

SIBO Breath Test

Lactulose and glucose breath testing to detect small intestinal bacterial overgrowth — the most commonly missed cause of IBS, bloating, and associated fatigue and brain fog.

Thyroid

Advanced Thyroid Panel

TSH, Free T3, Free T4, Reverse T3, TPO antibodies, and thyroglobulin antibodies — the complete picture that identifies subclinical hypothyroidism and Hashimoto's missed by standard TSH alone.

Nutrients

Intracellular Nutrient Panel

Intracellular (not serum) levels of magnesium, zinc, B vitamins, CoQ10, omega-3 index, vitamin D, and ferritin. Reveals deficiencies that standard blood tests routinely miss.

Food Sensitivities

IgG Food Sensitivity Testing

Delayed immune reactions (IgG) to 200+ foods — distinct from IgE allergies. Identifies food triggers driving inflammation, gut symptoms, joint pain, skin conditions, and fatigue.
