## Google My Business (GMB) Optimisation — Quick Wins

GMB is the single highest-ROI channel for local clinic visibility. Implement these immediately:

### Profile Completeness Checklist
- [ ] Category: "Integrative Medicine Physician" + "Holistic Medicine Practitioner" + "Alternative Medicine Practitioner"
- [ ] Secondary categories: based on services offered (Acupuncturist, Osteopath, Nutritionist, etc.)
- [ ] Description: 750-character description with top keywords (see template below)
- [ ] Services list: Every individual therapy as a named service with description
- [ ] Hours: Accurate + holiday hours
- [ ] Attributes: "Identifies as women-led", "Wheelchair accessible", "Online appointments" (if applicable)
- [ ] Photos: Minimum 20 high-quality photos (exterior, interior, reception, treatment rooms, practitioners)
- [ ] Q&A: Pre-seed 10 common patient questions with answers
- [ ] Posts: At minimum 1 GMB post per week (health tips, team introductions, patient stories)

### GMB Description Template
```
Insight Clinic is Islington's integrative health centre, combining conventional medicine 
with evidence-based complementary therapies to treat the root cause of illness — not just 
symptoms.

Based at Chapel Market, N1, our team of practitioners offer acupuncture, osteopathy, 
nutritional therapy, naturopathy, and integrative GP consultations under one roof.

Whether you're managing a chronic condition, recovering from injury, or simply ready to 
take a proactive approach to your health — we're here to help.

Book your initial consultation today.
```
