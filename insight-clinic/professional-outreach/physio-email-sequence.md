# Physio / Osteopath / Sports Therapist Outreach
**Target:** Physiotherapists, osteopaths, sports therapists, chiropractors in Islington and adjacent areas
**Why they refer:** Patients with chronic pain who don't fully resolve, or patients whose pain has nutritional/systemic roots

---

## How to Find Them

- Search "physiotherapist Islington", "osteopath Angel", "sports therapy N1"
- Check the Chartered Society of Physiotherapy directory (csp.org.uk/find-a-physio)
- Check the General Osteopathic Council directory (osteopathy.org.uk)
- Look for independent clinics (not chains) — they have more referral autonomy

```

sessions, done the exercises, but their pain persists — or keeps coming
back. Often there's an underlying factor: gut inflammation, nutritional
deficiency, adrenal stress, or a systemic issue that's driving the tissue
response.

We bring acupuncture, nutritional therapy, and naturopathy to bear on
exactly those cases. We often find the missing piece.

We're also a useful hand-off for:
• Patients who've completed their physio course but want ongoing support
• Patients dealing with stress/anxiety alongside their physical symptoms
• Patients who ask you about nutrition, sleep, or systemic approaches
  (and you'd rather refer than try to answer)

I'd love to have a brief chat — or if it's easier, I can drop by the
practice one morning and introduce myself properly.

Would [specific date, time] work? Or let me know what suits you.

Best,

[Your name]
Insight Clinic · insightclinic.care · [phone]
```

## In-Person Visit Script

Physios and osteopaths often respond better to a drop-in than email.
Visit between 8–9am or 12–1pm (between appointments).

> "Hi — I'm [name] from Insight Clinic just down the road at Chapel Market.
> I'm not here to sell anything, just to introduce ourselves. We see a lot
> of patients with chronic pain and musculoskeletal issues and we often
> work really well alongside physio practices. I've left a few of our
> referral cards — if you ever have a patient who might benefit from an
> acupuncture or nutrition assessment, we'd love to help. And happy to
> return the favour — we refer back when patients need hands-on work."

Leave: printed referral guide + business cards.
