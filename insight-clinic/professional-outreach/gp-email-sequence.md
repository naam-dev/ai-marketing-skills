# GP Outreach Email Sequence
**Target:** NHS and private GPs in Islington, Angel, Highbury, Canonbury, Holloway
**Goal:** Establish a referral relationship for patients with chronic/complex conditions

---

## How to Find GPs to Contact

**NHS GP practices in Islington (start here):**
Search NHS.uk/find-a-gp — filter by Islington postcode areas:
- N1 (Angel, Islington, Barnsbury)
- N7 (Holloway, Caledonian Road)
- N5 (Highbury)
- N4 (Finsbury Park)
- EC1V (Clerkenwell, adjacent)

**Private GP clinics in the area:**
- Search "private GP Islington" and "private GP Angel London"
- These are often more receptive — their patients already pay privately for healthcare

**Who to address:**
- GP practices: address the Practice Manager (they handle external correspondence) or the lead GP
- Private clinics: address the clinic director or individual GPs directly
- Find names on their websites — personalised emails convert 3x better than "Dear Doctor"

## Email 1 — Cold Introduction

**Subject line options (A/B test these):**

- A: `Integrative health referral pathway — Insight Clinic, Islington`
- B: `For your complex patients who need more than standard care`
- C: `[Practice name] + Insight Clinic — referral partnership`

**Use subject B for private GPs, A or C for NHS practices.**

---

## Response Handling

| Response type | What to do |
|---|---|
| Interested — wants a call | Book it within 48 hours. Bring a printed referral guide. |
| Interested — wants more info | Send the referral guide PDF + offer a practice visit |
| Not interested right now | Reply warmly, ask if you can follow up in 3 months |
| No reply after 3 emails | Move on. Add to a "6-month re-touch" list |
| Referral comes in | Call the patient within 24 hours. Send the GP a written outcome summary after the first appointment — this is what turns one referral into an ongoing stream |

## After a Referral: Closing the Loop

This is the step most clinics miss. When a GP refers a patient and hears nothing back, they stop referring.

**Send this within 48 hours of the patient's first appointment:**

```
Subject: Re: [Patient first name only] — outcome summary from Insight Clinic

Dear [GP name],

Thank you for referring [Patient first name] to us. I wanted to close the
loop following their initial consultation on [date].

[2–3 sentences summarising what you found, what you're treating, and your
plan — keep it clinical and concise. Example:]

"We identified [x] as a likely contributing factor alongside [y]. We've
commenced a course of [treatment] and have requested [test] to clarify
[issue]. We'll review in [timeframe] and will keep you updated on progress."

Please don't hesitate to contact me if you have any questions or if there
are any updates from your end that would be helpful for us to know.

Kind regards,

[Your name]
[Registration number / body]
Insight Clinic · insightclinic.care
```

This one habit — sending outcome summaries — will make you the integrative clinic that GPs actively recommend, because you make them look good to their patients and you keep them in the loop.
