## Instructions for Printing

- **Format:** A4, portrait, single-sided
- **Recommended:** Print on 170gsm card stock for a professional feel
- **Quantity:** Print 50–100 initially. Leave 5–10 per referral partner visit.
- **Optional:** Add your clinic logo to the top-left before printing
- **PDF tip:** To convert this to a formatted PDF, paste the content into Canva or a Word document and use their PDF export. Canva has free healthcare flyer templates you can adapt.

---

## What to Fill In Before Printing

Replace the bracketed placeholders:
- `[YOUR PHONE NUMBER]` — your main clinic number
- `[YOUR EMAIL]` — your general enquiries email
- Update the services list to match exactly what you offer (remove any that don't apply)
- Update the regulatory bodies listed to match your practitioners' actual registrations
- If you don't have an integrative GP, remove that line
