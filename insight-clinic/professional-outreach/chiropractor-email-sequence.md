# Chiropractor Outreach
**Target:** GCC-registered chiropractors in Islington, Angel, Highbury, Holloway, Clerkenwell
**Why they refer:** Chiro patients with recurring or non-resolving symptoms often have systemic/nutritional drivers — inflammation, gut issues, adrenal load — that spinal adjustment alone won't fix

---

## How to Find Them

- General Chiropractic Council directory: gcc-uk.org/find-a-chiropractor
- Search "chiropractor Islington", "chiropractor Angel London", "chiropractor N1"
- British Chiropractic Association directory: chiropractic-uk.co.uk
- Check Google Maps for N1, N7, N5 postcodes

---

## The Referral Angle

Chiropractors and integrative clinics complement each other well because:
- Chiro addresses structural issues; you address the systemic drivers of inflammation and tissue health
- Patients who relapse repeatedly after adjustment often have a nutritional or gut-health root cause
- Nutritional therapy (anti-inflammatory diet, omega-3, collagen, magnesium) supports the structural work
- Acupuncture for pain management sits naturally alongside chiro treatment

The pitch: **"We handle what's driving the inflammation. You handle the structure. Together patients get better faster and stay better longer."**

## In-Person Drop-In Script

Chiropractors often work in small independent practices and are very
approachable in person. Try a morning drop-in (before 9am or 12–1pm).

> "Hi, I'm [name] from Insight Clinic just down on Chapel Market —
> integrative health centre. I won't take much of your time. We work
> alongside a few chiro practices in the area — we look at the
> nutritional and systemic side of things for patients who keep
> relapsing, so your structural work holds better. Just wanted to
> introduce ourselves and leave a referral card. Happy to refer back
> to you too — do you have a card?"

Leave: printed referral guide + business cards.

## The Mutual Referral Offer

Chiropractors respond well to reciprocity. Be explicit:

- When a patient presents to you with musculoskeletal symptoms, refer them to the local chiropractor for structural assessment
- Tell the chiropractor you're doing this — make the two-way nature of the relationship clear from the start
- This dramatically increases the likelihood they refer back
