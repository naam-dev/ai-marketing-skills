## Status Key

| Status | Meaning | Next Action |
|---|---|---|
| `Not contacted` | On list, not yet emailed | Send Email 1 |
| `Email 1 sent` | First email sent | Follow up Day 7 |
| `Email 2 sent` | Follow-up sent | Final touch Day 21 |
| `Email 3 sent` | Final email sent | Move to 6-month re-touch |
| `Replied — interested` | They responded positively | Book call/visit within 48h |
| `Replied — not now` | Politely declined | Re-contact in 3–6 months |
| `Call booked` | Meeting scheduled | Prepare, bring referral guide |
| `Meeting done` | Visit/call completed | Send thank-you same day |
| `Active referrer` | Has sent at least 1 patient | Nurture monthly, send outcome summaries |
| `Unresponsive` | 3 emails, no reply | 6-month re-touch |

## Weekly Outreach Rhythm

**Monday:**
- Review tracker
- Send any overdue follow-ups (Email 2 or 3)
- Add 3 new contacts to the list

**Wednesday:**
- Send Email 1 to 3 new contacts
- Respond to any replies received

**Friday:**
- Log any referrals received this week and which partner sent them
- Send outcome summaries to any referring partner whose patient was seen this week

---

## Monthly Review

At the end of each month, answer:
1. How many contacts emailed? (Target: 15–20/month across all partner types)
2. How many replied? (Expect 15–25% reply rate)
3. How many meetings/calls had? (Expect 30–50% of replies)
4. How many referrals received? (Build over 3–6 months)
5. Which partner type is converting best? (GP / Osteopath / Chiro / Pilates / Yoga / Therapist)
6. Which email subject line performed better?
7. How many workshops/talks delivered? (Target: 1/month by Month 2)

## 90-Day Outreach Targets

| Month | Contacts emailed | Meetings | Workshops | New referral partners | Referrals received |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Month 1 | 20 | 4–6 | 0 | 2–3 | 0–3 |
| Month 2 | 20 | 4–6 | 1 | 3–5 | 3–8 |
| Month 3 | 15 (maintenance) | 3–4 | 1–2 | 2–3 | 8–15 |

By Month 3, a small number of active referrers sending even 1–2 patients/month each can represent a significant share of your new patient volume.

---

## Partner Type Priority Order

Work through these in order — highest referral conversion at the top:

1. **Osteopaths** — shared philosophy, easiest to convert, highest quality referrals
2. **Counsellors/therapists** — strong overlap in patient type, motivated to have a clinical partner
3. **GPs (private)** — high patient volume, more autonomy than NHS
4. **Pilates studios** — health-conscious clients, workshop format works very well
5. **Chiropractors** — good overlap, mutual referral dynamic
6. **Yoga studios** — larger reach, slower to formalise but high volume potential via workshops
7. **Physiotherapists** — useful but more referral inertia than osteopaths
8. **GPs (NHS)** — highest volume potential but slowest and hardest to convert
