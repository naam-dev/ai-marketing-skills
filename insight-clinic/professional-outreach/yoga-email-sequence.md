# Yoga Studio Outreach
**Target:** Yoga studios, independent yoga teachers, yoga wellness centres in Islington, Angel, Highbury, Canonbury, Dalston
**Why they refer:** Yoga communities are deeply health-conscious, trust-driven, and word-of-mouth led — one studio relationship can unlock dozens of patients

---

## How to Find Them

- Search "yoga studio Islington", "yoga Angel London", "yoga N1", "yoga Highbury"
- Google Maps — N1, N5, N7, N16 postcodes
- Instagram: #islingtonyoga #angelyoga #londonwellness — find local studios by hashtag
- Mindbody / ClassPass listings for Islington-area yoga
- Treatwell for wellness-oriented yoga studios

**Key studios to research in the area (verify still operating):**
- Search specifically for: Hot yoga, Iyengar yoga, Ashtanga, Yin yoga, and yoga therapy studios — these tend to attract health-conscious students who invest in their wellbeing beyond the mat

**Who to contact:**
- Studio owner or director (primary decision-maker)
- Studio manager for larger spaces
- For independent teachers: reach them directly on Instagram or via their website

## The Referral Angle

Yoga communities and integrative health overlap almost perfectly:

- Yoga students are already invested in mind-body wellness
- They self-select as open to holistic and integrative approaches
- Teachers have deep, trust-based relationships with their students and hear about their health struggles regularly
- Studios often host workshops, talks, and wellness events — a natural fit for your practitioners
- The yoga ethos (treating the whole person, preventive health, lifestyle as medicine) aligns with integrative medicine philosophy

**The pitch:** "We share the same values — treating the whole person. When your students need clinical support for what's showing up on and off the mat, we're here."

## Workshop Topics for Yoga Audiences

These perform well with yoga communities — practical, values-aligned, not salesy:

| Topic | Why it lands | Best format |
|---|---|---|
| "The gut-brain axis — what yoga students need to know" | Gut health is huge in wellness communities; bridges to their practice | 40-min talk + Q&A |
| "Why you're exhausted even though you're doing everything right" | Speaks directly to over-extended, high-functioning yoga practitioners | 30-min talk |
| "Eating for energy, hormones, and recovery" | Women-dominated audience often managing hormonal issues | 45-min talk + handout |
| "The stress-inflammation connection — and what to do about it" | Resonates with urban London demographic | 35-min talk |
| "Ayurveda meets integrative medicine" | Bridges Eastern wellness philosophy with clinical practice | 60-min workshop |

**Workshop logistics:**
- Offer to run during a regular slot (e.g., after a Sunday morning class)
- Or as a standalone evening event (7–8:30pm works well for London yoga audiences)
- Bring: business cards, printed referral guide, email sign-up sheet, optional lead magnet PDF
- Target: 15–30 attendees → typically 2–4 bookings per workshop

## Social Media Collaboration

Yoga studios have engaged Instagram followings — a single story mention from a well-followed studio can drive meaningful traffic:

**Easy collab formats:**
- **Instagram takeover:** You take over their Stories for a day with health tips
- **Joint post:** "Ask an integrative health practitioner" — they post a health question from their community, you answer it
- **Co-created Reel:** "5 things your body is telling you on the mat" (physical signs that may have clinical root causes)
- **Cross-promotion:** They post about you, you post about them — simple swap

**How to propose it:**

> "Would you be open to a story swap? We'd love to share your studio
> with our clinic community, and if you're happy to mention us to
> yours — even just a 'local health partner we trust' — that would
> mean a lot. No cost, just local businesses supporting each other."

## The Community Angle

Yoga studios are community hubs. The relationship goes beyond referrals:

- **Sponsor a community class:** Offer to sponsor a free community yoga session — your clinic name on the event, you attend and meet students
- **Donation to a cause:** Co-host a charity yoga class (e.g., mental health charity) — good PR for both businesses
- **Staff wellness:** Offer studio staff a discounted first consultation — they become advocates if they have a good experience
