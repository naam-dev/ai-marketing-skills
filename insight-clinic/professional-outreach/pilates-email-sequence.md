# Pilates Studio Outreach
**Target:** Pilates studios, independent Pilates instructors, clinical Pilates practitioners in Islington, Angel, Highbury, Canonbury
**Why they refer:** Pilates clients are health-conscious, proactive, and often managing chronic conditions — they self-select as ideal integrative medicine patients

- Search "Pilates studio Islington", "Pilates Angel London", "Pilates N1", "clinical Pilates Islington"
- Google Maps — look for independent studios (not chain gyms)
- Instagram search: #islingtonpilates #angelpilates #londonpilates
- Check local business directories (Yell, Bark.com)
- Treatwell listings for Pilates in N1/N7/N5

**Who to contact:**
- Studio owner / lead instructor (they make referral decisions)
- For larger studios: the studio manager
- For solo instructors: contact them directly — they're often very open

## The Referral Angle

Pilates clients and integrative medicine patients overlap heavily:

- Health-conscious and proactive about their wellbeing
- Often managing an injury, chronic pain, or post-surgical rehab
- Frequently ask their Pilates instructor about nutrition, fatigue, gut issues, hormones
- Instructors regularly hear: "I'm exhausted all the time", "my gut has been awful", "I just can't shift this weight"

**The pitch:** "Your clients trust you with their body. When they bring you health questions you can't answer, send them to us. We'll look after them — and send them back to you."

This framing makes the studio look better, not like they're handing off clients.

## The Health Talk Strategy

A 30–45 minute free talk at a Pilates studio is one of the highest-ROI
patient acquisition activities for an integrative clinic:

**Why it works:**
- You're speaking to a room full of health-conscious people who already invest in their wellbeing
- Trust is pre-built through the studio relationship
- You can collect emails ("sign up for our free fatigue guide")
- Typically 2–5 bookings from a single talk of 15–20 people

**Best talk topics for Pilates audiences:**
1. "Why you're exhausted — the 5 root causes your GP hasn't checked"
2. "Gut health: how your digestion affects your energy, mood, and pain"
3. "Hormones and your body — what's happening in your 30s and 40s"
4. "Anti-inflammatory eating for injury recovery and chronic pain"

**Talk format:**
- 25–30 minutes presentation (practical, not a sales pitch)
- 10 minutes Q&A
- 5 minutes: "If you'd like to explore this further, here's how to book a consultation"
- Leave printed clinic cards and a sign-up sheet

**Studio pitch for the talk:**
> "We'd love to offer your clients a free 30-minute health talk on
> [topic]. No cost to you or them — just useful content. We've done
> this at other studios in the area and clients really appreciate it.
> It would be a great addition to one of your regular class slots or
> an evening event."
