**Target:** Counsellors, psychotherapists, psychologists, CBT therapists in Islington

## Why This Relationship Works Both Ways

Many patients in therapy also have physical symptoms — fatigue, gut issues, poor sleep, anxiety with physical manifestations. Integrative medicine addresses the body side while therapy addresses the mind side. Counsellors who can say "I work alongside a clinic that looks at the physical side of what you're experiencing" become more valuable to their clients.

You can also refer to therapists — patients with chronic illness often develop anxiety or depression around their condition, and a referral to a trusted therapist strengthens both practices.

## Networking Alternative: CPD / Local Therapist Groups

Many therapists attend CPD events and peer supervision groups. Consider:

1. **Offer a free CPD talk:** "The gut-brain axis: what therapists need to know" — 45 minutes, presented at a local therapist group. Contact BACP local groups or therapy networks in Islington.

2. **Attend local BACP networking events** as the "integrative medicine partner" in the room.

3. **Write a short article** for a therapist newsletter or blog: "When your client's anxiety has a physical root" — positions you as the expert they refer to.
