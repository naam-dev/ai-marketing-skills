# 🏃 Physiotherapist Email Sequence

**Back to:** [[000-Insight-Clinic-MOC]] · [[Outreach-Tracker]]

**Priority:** 🟠 High — patients who plateau after physio are a high-signal referral trigger

**Where to find them:** csp.org.uk/find-a-physio · Google "physiotherapist Islington" · "sports therapy N1" · independent clinics preferred over chains

---

> [!tip] The ideal referral patient
> Patients who've completed a physio course, done the exercises, but whose pain persists or keeps returning. Often an underlying driver: gut inflammation, nutritional deficiency, adrenal stress, or systemic issue. That's where we come in — we often find the missing piece.

---

## Email 1

**Subject options:**
- A: `For your patients who plateau — integrative support from Insight Clinic` ← best
- B: `Insight Clinic, Islington — complementary referral pathway for physio patients`

```
Dear [Name],

I'm [Your name] from Insight Clinic, an integrative health centre at
Chapel Market, Islington (insightclinic.care).

I'm reaching out because I think our work complements what physio practices
like yours do well — and there's a specific patient type where we often see
strong outcomes working alongside physical therapy.

The patients I'm thinking of are the ones who plateau. They've had the
sessions, done the exercises, but their pain persists — or keeps coming
back. Often there's an underlying factor: gut inflammation, nutritional
deficiency, adrenal stress, or a systemic issue that's driving the tissue
response.

We bring acupuncture, nutritional therapy, and naturopathy to bear on
exactly those cases. We often find the missing piece.

We're also a useful hand-off for:
• Patients who've completed their physio course but want ongoing support
• Patients dealing with stress/anxiety alongside their physical symptoms
• Patients who ask you about nutrition, sleep, or systemic approaches
  (and you'd rather refer than try to answer)

I'd love to have a brief chat — or if it's easier, I can drop by the
practice one morning and introduce myself properly.

Would [specific date, time] work? Or let me know what suits you.

Best,

[Your name]
Insight Clinic · insightclinic.care · [phone]
```

## Email 2 — Follow-Up (Day 7)

```
Hi [Name],

Quick follow-up from last week. I know how busy practice life gets.

One thing that might be useful to know: when you refer a patient to us,
we send you a written summary after their first appointment. So you stay
in the loop — and the patient doesn't feel like they're disappearing
into a black hole.

Happy to do the same in reverse — if you'd ever like us to flag when a
patient might benefit from manual therapy alongside what we're doing,
we'll send them back your way.

Is there a better time to connect?

[Your name]
Insight Clinic
```

## In-Person Visit Script

> [!note] Drop-ins often beat emails with physios
> Visit between 8–9am or 12–1pm (between appointments). Physios and osteopaths often respond better to a face-to-face introduction.

> *"Hi — I'm [name] from Insight Clinic just down the road at Chapel Market.
> I'm not here to sell anything, just to introduce ourselves. We see a lot
> of patients with chronic pain and musculoskeletal issues and we often
> work really well alongside physio practices. I've left a few of our
> referral cards — if you ever have a patient who might benefit from an
> acupuncture or nutrition assessment, we'd love to help. And happy to
> return the favour — we refer back when patients need hands-on work."*
