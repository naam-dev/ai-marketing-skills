# 🧘‍♀️ Yoga Studio Email Sequence

**Back to:** [[000-Insight-Clinic-MOC]] · [[Outreach-Tracker]]

**Priority:** 🟡 Medium-High — larger reach, slower to formalise, but workshop format has very high volume potential

**Where to find them:** Google Maps · Mindbody · ClassPass · Instagram #islingtonyoga #angelpilates

> The relationship goes beyond referrals. Think: workshops, Instagram collabs, sponsored community classes. One well-connected studio can be worth 20 individual professional contacts.

## Email 1

**Subject options:**
- A: `Integrative health for your yoga community — Insight Clinic, Islington`
- B: `[Studio name] + Insight Clinic — whole-person wellness in N1` ← use if you know the studio

```
Dear [Name],

I'm [Your name] from Insight Clinic, an integrative health centre at
24 Chapel Market, Islington N1 9EZ (insightclinic.care).

Yoga students already understand the mind-body connection. They're 
health-conscious, proactive, and often dealing with things that show 
up in their practice: chronic fatigue, gut issues, hormonal changes, 
anxiety, persistent injury. Those are exactly the things we treat — 
at the clinical level.

I'd love to explore a few things:
• A simple mutual referral arrangement
• A free health workshop for your students (topics below)
• Any wellness events where we might contribute

Workshop topics that resonate with yoga audiences:
- "Why you're exhausted even though you're doing everything right"
- "The gut-brain axis — what yoga students need to know"
- "Hormones and your body — energy, mood, and your cycle"
- "The stress-inflammation connection — and what to do about it"

We're a 5-minute walk from Angel tube — genuinely local.

Would a coffee make sense? I'm happy to come to you.

[3 date/time options]

[Your name] · Insight Clinic · insightclinic.care · [phone]
```

## Community Angle (Beyond Referrals)

- **Sponsor a community class:** Your clinic name on the event, you attend and meet students
- **Co-host a charity yoga class:** Good PR for both businesses
- **Staff wellness offer:** Discounted first consultation for studio staff — they become advocates
- **Instagram Story swap:** Zero cost, both audiences see both brands
