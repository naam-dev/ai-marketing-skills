# 🦴 Osteopath Email Sequence

**Back to:** [[000-Insight-Clinic-MOC]] · [[Outreach-Tracker]]

**Priority:** 🔴 #1 — Start here. Most naturally aligned philosophy of any partner type. Easiest to convert.

**Where to find them:** osteopathy.org.uk/find-an-osteopath · "osteopath Islington" on Google

---

> [!tip] Why osteopaths first
> Osteopathy is already a whole-body philosophy. They understand systemic drivers. The referral language is natural and the relationship is genuinely two-way — you'll refer back to them too.

---

## Email 1 — Introduction

**Subject options:**
- A: `Fellow whole-body practitioners — Insight Clinic, Islington` ← best opener
- B: `Integrative health referral partnership — Insight Clinic, N1`
- C: `For your patients where there's more going on systemically`

```
Dear [Name],

I'm [Your name] from Insight Clinic, an integrative health centre at
24 Chapel Market, Islington N1 9EZ (insightclinic.care).

I wanted to reach out because I think osteopaths and integrative
clinics are natural partners — we're both looking at the whole person,
just from different directions.

You're treating the structure and fascial system. We're addressing the 
systemic load — gut health, nutrition, hormonal balance, inflammation — 
that so often underlies the musculoskeletal presentation.

For patients where you sense there's more going on beneath the surface,
we're a useful next step. Particularly for:

• Chronic or recurring musculoskeletal pain with suspected systemic drivers
• Patients with significant fatigue alongside their physical symptoms
• Hormonal presentations — perimenopausal patients with joint pain, PCOS
• Patients asking about nutrition or lifestyle that falls outside your scope
• Post-viral or Long COVID patients

We also refer back — when patients come to us with musculoskeletal 
complaints, we regularly recommend osteopathic assessment.

All our practitioners are registered with their relevant regulatory bodies
and we send written outcome summaries after referrals.

Would a brief call or coffee make sense? I'm just around the corner from Angel.

[3 specific date/time options]

Warm regards,
[Your name] · Insight Clinic · insightclinic.care · [phone]
```

## CPD Co-Hosting Opportunity

> [!note] One evening = multiple referral relationships
> This is the highest-leverage activity in the whole outreach programme. One 90-minute CPD evening can generate 5–10 referral relationships at once.

**Pitch to an osteopath you've connected with:**

> *"I'd love to explore co-hosting a CPD evening — something like 'The systemic drivers of musculoskeletal pain: a collaborative approach.' You'd present the structural perspective, I'd present the nutritional and inflammatory side. We could invite other local practitioners. Happy to organise it."*

**Format:** 90-minute evening · neutral venue or one of your clinics · invite physios, chiros, other osteopaths · both practices co-promote
