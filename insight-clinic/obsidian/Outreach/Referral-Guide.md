## Before Printing — Fill In These Placeholders

- `[YOUR PHONE NUMBER]` — main clinic number
- `[YOUR EMAIL]` — general enquiries email
- Review the services list and remove any that don't apply to your current team
- Update the regulatory bodies to match your practitioners' actual registrations
- If you don't have an integrative GP, remove that line

## Printing Instructions

| Setting | Value |
|---|---|
