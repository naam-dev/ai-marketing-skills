# 🩺 GP Email Sequence

**Back to:** [[000-Insight-Clinic-MOC]] · [[Outreach-Tracker]]

**Priority:** 🟠 High — highest long-term volume, but slowest to convert. Start after osteopaths and therapists.

**Where to find them:** nhs.uk/find-a-gp (NHS) · "private GP Islington" (Google)

---

## Email 1 — Cold Introduction

**Subject options:**
- A: `Integrative health referral pathway — Insight Clinic, Islington`
- B: `For your complex patients who need more than standard care` ← best for private GPs
- C: `[Practice name] + Insight Clinic — referral partnership`

```
Dear [Name / Practice Manager],

I'm reaching out from Insight Clinic, an integrative health centre based at
24 Chapel Market, Islington N1 9EZ (insightclinic.care).

We specialise in treating patients whose symptoms persist despite standard
care — using a root-cause approach that combines conventional clinical
assessment with evidence-based therapies including acupuncture, nutritional
therapy, osteopathy, and naturopathy.

We regularly see good outcomes for patients with:

• Chronic fatigue and post-viral fatigue (including Long COVID)
• Digestive conditions — IBS, bloating, food sensitivities, gut dysbiosis
• Hormonal imbalance — PCOS, perimenopause, thyroid dysfunction
• Anxiety, low mood, and stress-related conditions
• Musculoskeletal pain that hasn't resolved with standard physiotherapy
• Patients who want an integrative approach to managing a long-term condition

Our practitioners are all registered with their relevant regulatory bodies
[e.g., BAcC, BANT, GOsC], and we provide written outcome summaries after 
each patient's initial consultation so that you remain fully informed.

I'd welcome the chance to introduce ourselves properly. Would any of the 
following work for a 15-minute call?

[3 specific date/time options]

Many thanks,

[Your name] · [Your title]
Insight Clinic · insightclinic.care · [phone]
```

## After a Referral — Outcome Summary

> [!warning] This is the most important habit in the whole playbook
> Send this within 48 hours of the patient's first appointment. GPs who get outcome summaries refer again. GPs who hear nothing stop.

**Subject:** `Re: [Patient first name] — outcome summary from Insight Clinic`

```
Dear [GP name],

Thank you for referring [Patient first name]. I wanted to close the loop 
following their initial consultation on [date].

[2–3 sentences: what you found · what you're treating · your plan]

Example: "We identified low Free T3 and significant magnesium depletion 
as likely contributing factors. We've commenced a nutritional protocol 
and have requested a DUTCH hormone test to clarify the hormonal picture. 
We'll review in 6 weeks and will keep you updated."

Please don't hesitate to contact me if you have any questions.

Kind regards,
[Your name] · [Registration number]
Insight Clinic · insightclinic.care
```

## Response Handling

| Response | Action |
|---|---|
| Interested — wants a call | Book within 48 hours. Bring [[Referral-Guide]]. |
| Interested — wants more info | Send referral guide PDF + offer a practice visit |
| Not interested right now | Reply warmly, ask to follow up in 3 months |
| No reply after 3 emails | 6-month re-touch. Note in [[Outreach-Tracker]]. |
| Referral comes in | Call patient within 24 hours. Send outcome summary after first appointment. |
