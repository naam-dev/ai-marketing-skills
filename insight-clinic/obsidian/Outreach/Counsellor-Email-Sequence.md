# 🧠 Counsellor / Therapist Email Sequence

**Back to:** [[000-Insight-Clinic-MOC]] · [[Outreach-Tracker]]

**Priority:** 🔴 #2 — Strong patient overlap. Therapists who understand the gut-brain connection actively want a clinical partner.

**Where to find them:** bacp.co.uk/search · psychotherapy.org.uk · Psychology Today UK · "therapist Islington"

---

> [!tip] Key insight
> Many mental health presentations have a physiological root — thyroid, gut, nutrients, sleep. Therapists who know this refer because it makes them more effective, not less. Frame this as "we make your work go deeper, not replace it."

## Email 1

**Subject options:**
- A: `The body-mind connection — integrative health referral from Insight Clinic`
- B: `For your clients where physical symptoms are part of the picture` ← best

```
Dear [Name],

I'm [Your name] from Insight Clinic, an integrative health centre at
24 Chapel Market, Islington N1 9EZ (insightclinic.care).

I'm reaching out because we see real crossover between what you do 
and what we offer. The clients I'm thinking of are those where anxiety, 
low mood, or chronic stress is presenting alongside physical symptoms: 
fatigue, gut issues, poor sleep, brain fog, hormonal disruption.

These often have physiological roots — gut-brain axis dysregulation, 
HPA axis dysfunction, nutritional deficiencies — that therapy alone 
won't fully resolve.

We don't replace therapy. We address the body side so your work can 
go deeper.

We're also a useful referral for:
• Clients with longstanding fatigue or burnout who feel "stuck"
• Clients who've raised questions about diet, sleep, or physiology
  that fall outside your scope
• Clients where you suspect a physical component to their presentation

I'd love to explore whether a mutual referral relationship makes sense.
Happy to grab a coffee near the clinic, or speak briefly by phone.

[3 date/time options]

Warm regards,
[Your name] · Insight Clinic · insightclinic.care · [phone]
```
