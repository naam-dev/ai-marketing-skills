# 🧘 Pilates Studio Email Sequence

**Back to:** [[000-Insight-Clinic-MOC]] · [[Outreach-Tracker]]

**Priority:** 🟠 High — health-conscious audience, free health talk converts very well

**Where to find them:** Google Maps "Pilates studio Islington" · Treatwell · ClassPass · Instagram #islingtonpilates

---

> [!tip] Best opening move
> Lead with the free health talk offer. It's a concrete, low-commitment thing to say yes to — far easier than "let's set up a referral arrangement."

---

## Email 1

**Subject options:**
- A: `For your clients whose health questions go beyond Pilates — Insight Clinic, Islington` ← best
- B: `[Studio name] + Insight Clinic — a natural partnership`

```
Dear [Name],

I'm [Your name] from Insight Clinic, an integrative health centre at
24 Chapel Market, Islington (insightclinic.care).

Pilates studios and integrative health clinics attract the same people: 
those who take their health seriously. The clients I'm thinking of are 
the ones who come to Pilates but are clearly dealing with more — exhaustion, 
gut issues, hormones feeling off, told everything's fine but they don't feel fine.

Those are exactly the patients we help.

What this could look like in practice:
• You mention us when a client raises a health question you can't answer
• We keep your studio cards to recommend to patients who'd benefit from Pilates
• One of our practitioners offers a short health talk for your clients — 
  topics like "Why you're exhausted even though you're doing everything right" 
  or "Gut health and your body" go down very well with Pilates audiences

Would it be worth a quick chat to see if a partnership makes sense?

[3 date/time options]

[Your name] · Insight Clinic · insightclinic.care · [phone]
```

---

## Email 2 — Follow-Up (Day 7)

```
Hi [Name],

Quick follow-up from last week.

We actively recommend Pilates to our patients — particularly for recovery, 
chronic pain, and rebuilding strength after illness. Having a local studio 
we trust would make a real difference to that recommendation.

Is there a better time to connect?

[Your name] · Insight Clinic
```

## Email 3 — Final (Day 21)

```
Hi [Name],

Last note. The health talk offer stands — topics like "Why you're 
exhausted all the time", "Gut health and your body", or "Hormones and 
energy" go down really well with Pilates audiences. No cost, no 
obligation — just useful content for your clients.

Referral guide attached.

[Your name] · Insight Clinic · [phone]
```

## Health Talk — Quick Pitch

Use this in person or at the end of a call:

> *"Would you be open to us doing a free 30-minute health talk for your clients? We've done these at other studios and they really appreciate the practical content. We'd keep it completely non-commercial — just useful health information. We could slot it after a class or as a standalone evening event."*
