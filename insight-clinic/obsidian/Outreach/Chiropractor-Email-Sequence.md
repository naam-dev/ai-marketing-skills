# 🦷 Chiropractor Email Sequence

**Back to:** [[000-Insight-Clinic-MOC]] · [[Outreach-Tracker]]

**Priority:** 🟠 High — structural + systemic overlap is natural. Recurring patients are the ideal referral signal.

**Where to find them:** gcc-uk.org/find-a-chiropractor · chiropractic-uk.co.uk · Google Maps N1, N7, N5 postcodes

---

> [!tip] The pitch in one sentence
> "We handle what's driving the inflammation. You handle the structure. Together patients get better faster and stay better longer."
>
> Patients who relapse repeatedly after adjustment often have a nutritional or gut-health root cause — dysbiosis, omega-3 deficiency, high cortisol, food-driven inflammation. That's our lane.

## Email 1

**Subject options:**
- A: `For your patients who keep coming back — integrative support from Insight Clinic` ← best
- B: `Insight Clinic, Islington — complementary referral for chiro patients`
- C: `What's driving the inflammation? Integrative medicine for your recurring cases`

```
Dear [Name],

I'm [Your name] from Insight Clinic, an integrative health centre at
24 Chapel Market, Islington N1 9EZ (insightclinic.care).

I wanted to introduce ourselves because I think there's real overlap
between what you do and what we offer — particularly for patients who
keep coming back with recurring symptoms despite good chiropractic care.

We often see a nutritional or systemic root to those cases: chronic
inflammation driven by gut dysbiosis, food sensitivities, omega-3
deficiency, or high cortisol. Once you address that, the structural
work holds so much better.

We're useful for your patients who:

• Relapse regularly after adjustment and you suspect there's something
  systemic driving it
• Have significant inflammation that's slowing their progress
• Ask about diet, supplements, or lifestyle alongside their treatment
• Present with fatigue, gut issues, or hormonal symptoms alongside
  their musculoskeletal complaint

We offer acupuncture, nutritional therapy, and naturopathy — all from
registered practitioners. We don't touch the structural work; that's
yours. We just address what's underneath it.

After any referral, we'll send you a written summary of our findings
so you stay in the loop.

Would it be worth a brief call or a coffee to see if a referral
relationship makes sense? I'm just around the corner.

[3 specific date/time options]

Best,

[Your name]
Insight Clinic · insightclinic.care · [phone]
```

## The Mutual Referral Offer

Be explicit about reciprocity from the start — chiropractors respond very well to it:

- When patients present with musculoskeletal symptoms, refer them to the local chiropractor for structural assessment
- Tell the chiropractor you're doing this — name the two-way nature of the relationship from the first conversation
- This dramatically increases the likelihood they refer back
