## Weekly Rhythm

**Monday:**
- Review tracker — any overdue follow-ups?

**Wednesday:**
- Send Email 1 to 3 new contacts
- Respond to any replies

**Friday:**
- Log referrals received this week
- Send outcome summaries for patients seen this week → [[GP-Email-Sequence#After a Referral]]
