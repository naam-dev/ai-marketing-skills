## Compliance Checklist

Run through this before every post:

- [ ] No specific cure claims for named conditions (ASA + MHRA both enforce this)
- [ ] Patient stories: anonymised + written consent on file — no exceptions
- [ ] Before/after photos require signed model release
- [ ] BAcC code: acupuncture claims in line with published evidence summaries
- [ ] Dr Ravi's title: "Integrative Medicines Physician" — not "GP" unless currently GMC-registered
- [ ] Pricing shows standard fee — no strikethrough "discounts" unless genuinely time-limited
- [ ] All medical content visibly authored or reviewed by a named practitioner
