### Typography
- **Primary:** Barlow Condensed (headers, slide copy, strong statements)
- **Secondary:** Clean sans-serif for body copy

### Photography Style
- Natural, warm lighting with a hint of steel blue from windows
- Hands-only treatment shots (no identifiable faces without consent)
- Clinic details: herbs, tinctures, dispensary, couch, exterior
- Mood: calm · professional · established · deeply experienced

### Video Style (Reels)
- 9:16 vertical format for all Reels and Stories
- Slow, deliberate movement — nothing rushed or energetic
- Ambient sound preferred over trending audio (timeless > trendy)
- Colour grading: warm cream + steel blue combination
- See [[Higgsfield-Scripts]] for all AI video prompts

## Brand Voice

**Tone:** Authoritative but warm. Clinical but human. Never salesy, never sensationalist.

**What Insight sounds like:**
- "We don't treat conditions. We look for what's underneath them."
- "Forty years of pattern recognition."
- "It's not Botox. It's not filler. It's your own body, reminded."
- "Most of what's online is wrong. Here's what's actually true."

**What Insight never sounds like:**
- Hype, miracle claims, before/after transformations
- Anything that implies cure of a named condition
- Urgency manufactured from thin air
- Generic wellness platitudes

## Compliance Framework

### Regulatory bodies to stay inside:
- **ASA** (Advertising Standards Authority) — no specific cure claims for named conditions
- **MHRA** — no unlicensed medicinal claims
- **BAcC code** — acupuncture claims must align with published evidence summaries
- **GCC** — if chiropractors ever featured

### Standing rules:
- No specific cure claims for named conditions
- Patient stories: anonymised + written consent on file
- Before/after photos: signed model release required
- Dr Ravi's title: "Integrative Medicines Physician" — confirm GMC status before using "GP"
- Pricing: standard fee shown, no fake strikethrough discounts
- All medical content: visibly authored or reviewed by a named practitioner
- Avoid "energy" in Instagram captions — the algorithm flags wellness energy claims. Use "rhythm", "regulation", "restoration" instead.
