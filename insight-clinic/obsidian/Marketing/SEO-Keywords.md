## Google My Business Optimisation

> [!warning] Highest ROI action — do this first
> GMB is the #1 patient acquisition channel for a local clinic. Implement all of this before anything else.

### Checklist
- [ ] Primary category: "Integrative Medicine Physician" or "Alternative Medicine Practitioner"
- [ ] Add every service individually (acupuncture, osteopathy, nutrition, etc.)
- [ ] Write 750-char description (template below)
- [ ] Upload 20+ photos (exterior, interior, treatment rooms, team)
- [ ] Add booking link
- [ ] Set accurate hours + holiday hours
- [ ] Pre-seed Q&A with 10 common patient questions
- [ ] Post 3x/week (Mon: health tip · Wed: service · Fri: availability + booking nudge)

### GMB Description Template
```
Insight Clinic is Islington's integrative health centre, combining conventional 
medicine with evidence-based complementary therapies to treat the root cause of 
illness — not just symptoms.

Based at Chapel Market, N1, our team offers acupuncture, osteopathy, nutritional 
therapy, naturopathy, and integrative GP consultations under one roof.

Whether you're managing a chronic condition, recovering from injury, or simply 
ready to take a proactive approach to your health — we're here to help.

Book your initial consultation today.
```
