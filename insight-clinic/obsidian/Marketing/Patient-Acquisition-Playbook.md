## Channel 1 — Google My Business

> [!tip] Single highest-ROI action
> Fully completing your GMB profile + 20 reviews will move you to the top 3 of local search within 4–8 weeks. Do this before anything else.

Full optimisation checklist → [[SEO-Keywords#Google My Business Optimisation]]

**Review generation — the ask:**
Train front desk to say after every satisfied appointment:
> *"We'd really appreciate a Google review — it helps other Islington locals find us."*

Follow up with this text/email within 24 hours:

```
Subject: How was your appointment at Insight Clinic?

Hi [Name],

Thank you for visiting us. If you're happy with your experience, 
we'd be grateful if you could leave us a Google review — it only 
takes 2 minutes and really helps other people in Islington find care that works.

[LEAVE A REVIEW → direct link]

Thank you,
[Practitioner name]
```

**Respond to every review within 48 hours** — positive and negative.

## Channel 2 — Patient Referrals

> [!note] The moment to ask
> When a patient says "I feel so much better" — that's when to ask. Not at the end, not in an email. In the moment.

**The verbal ask:**
> *"I'm really glad that's helped you. If you know anyone struggling with something similar, please send them our way — we'd love to help."*

**Referral incentive options:**

| Option | Structure | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| Double-sided discount | £30 off for new patient + £30 credit for referrer | High volume |
| Thank-you card | Handwritten card when referral books | Premium/personal feel |
| No incentive | Strong word of mouth with a great patient experience | When experience speaks for itself |

**Tracking:**
- Add "How did you hear about us?" to the booking form
- If "referred by a patient" → collect their name → apply credit

## Channel 4 — Google Ads

**Budget:** Start at £15/day (£450/month). Scale what converts.

### Campaign Structure

**Campaign 1: Brand protection** — £50/month
- Keywords: "Insight Clinic", "insightclinic.care"

**Campaign 2: Integrative Health — Local** — £300–600/month
```
"integrative health clinic Islington"
"integrative medicine London"
"holistic health clinic near me"
"naturopath Islington"
"acupuncture Angel Islington"
"nutritionist Islington"
```

**Campaign 3: Condition-specific** — £200–400/month
```
"chronic fatigue specialist London"
"IBS treatment London"
"PCOS treatment London"
"hormonal imbalance doctor London"
```

### Ad Copy Template
```
Headline 1: Integrative Health Clinic — Islington
Headline 2: Treat the Root Cause, Not Just Symptoms
Headline 3: Book Your Consultation Today

Description 1: Chapel Market, N1. Evidence-based integrative medicine.
Acupuncture, nutrition, osteopathy & more. Same-week appointments.

Description 2: Tired of being told your results are normal? Our team 
finds what conventional medicine misses. Book a consultation today.
```

> [!warning] Landing page matters
> Do NOT send ad clicks to the homepage. Use `website/additional-pages/new-patients.html` as your landing page — single CTA, no nav distractions.
