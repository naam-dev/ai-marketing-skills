# 🎬 Higgsfield Video Scripts

**Back to:** [[000-Insight-Clinic-MOC]] · [[Social-Calendar-MayJun2026]]

> [!tip] How to use these
> Paste each prompt directly into Higgsfield. Generate 2–3 variations and pick the strongest. Use 9:16 aspect ratio for all Reels and Stories.

---

## Workflow

1. Paste prompt into Higgsfield → generate 2–3 variations
2. Download best version
3. Open in **CapCut** or **Canva** → add text overlays + Insight logo end-card (~10 min per video)
4. Add soft royalty-free music from **Epidemic Sound** or **Artlist** — avoid trending audio (dates fast, rarely fits the clinic tone)
5. Export at **1080×1920** for Instagram Reels/Stories and Facebook Reels

## Script 1 · Website Launch Montage

**For:** [[Social-Calendar-MayJun2026]] Thu 28 May
**Duration:** 8–12s

```
Cinematic morning street in North London, soft golden light, slow tracking
shot approaching a frosted-glass clinic doorway with subtle brass signage
reading 'Insight'. Camera glides through into a warm, minimal reception
with a single steel-blue accent wall, lingers on a small bowl of dried
herbs and a stack of leaflets, then cuts to a hand opening a laptop
showing a clean clinical website homepage. Mood: calm, professional,
established. Steel blue and warm cream colour grading. No people's faces.
9:16 vertical.
```

## Script 2 · Iris Macro — "3 Things"

**For:** [[Social-Calendar-MayJun2026]] Sat 6 Jun
**Duration:** 12–15s

```
Extreme macro close-up of a human iris, cinematic, high detail, slowly
rotating. Three soft colour overlays cycle one after another — first a
warm gold wash, then a steel-blue wash, then a deep navy wash. Each shift
is gentle, accompanied by a subtle focus pull. The iris feels alive,
almost geological in detail. Calm, contemplative mood. No text overlays

```

## Script 3 · Dr Ravi Cinematic Intro

**For:** [[Social-Calendar-MayJun2026]] Mon 2 Jun
**Duration:** 10s

```
Soft cinematic portrait of a distinguished South Asian male physician in
his late 60s, in a warmly lit clinical office, salt-and-pepper hair,
wearing a smart charcoal jacket over an open-collar shirt. He is
mid-conversation, listening — not speaking. Slight smile, full attention.
The room behind him is out of focus: bookshelves, anatomical illustration
on the wall, an antique brass desk lamp. Mood: trustworthy, deeply
experienced. Warm tungsten colour grading with a hint of steel blue from
a window. 9:16 vertical, very shallow depth of field.
```

> [!note] Real photo preferred
> This is a stand-in if you can't shoot Dr Ravi in-person quickly. Always prefer a real photograph of the actual practitioner — it builds far more trust.

## Additional Scripts for Future Posts

These can be generated as needed for ongoing content:

### Dispensary / Metagenics
```
Slow close-up pan across a clean clinic dispensary shelf: glass bottles
of herbal tinctures, a mortar and pestle, neatly arranged supplement
boxes with clean modern labels. Warm natural light from the left, steel
blue accent wall behind. Hand reaches into frame and gently picks up
one bottle, holds it up to the light. Mood: quality, precision, care.
No faces. 9:16 vertical.
```

### Chapel Market Exterior — Morning
```
Early morning establishing shot of a North London market street, golden
hour light, few people about. Camera slowly approaches a discrete clinic
entrance — frosted glass, brass fittings, small plaque. The street has
the character of somewhere real — not sterile. Mood: local, rooted,
established. 9:16 vertical.
```

### Herbs + Homeopathy
```
Macro close-up of dried herbs in an antique ceramic bowl, soft natural
light, depth of field pulling slowly to reveal a practitioner's hands
writing notes in a leather-bound journal. Warm tones throughout, no
clinical white. Mood: considered, traditional craft with modern clinical
depth. 9:16 vertical.
```
