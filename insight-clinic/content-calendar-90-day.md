# Insight Clinic — 90-Day Content Calendar
**Goal:** Attract new patients through organic search + social proof
**Channels:** Website blog, Google My Business posts, Instagram, Email newsletter
**Publishing cadence:** 2 blog posts/month · 3 GMB posts/week · 4 Instagram posts/week · 1 email newsletter/month

## Month 1: Establish Authority & Local Presence

### Week 1 (Days 1–7)

**Blog Post 1:**
- Title: "What Is Integrative Medicine — And Why Islington Patients Are Choosing It"
- Target keyword: `integrative medicine London` / `integrative health clinic Islington`
- Word count: 1,400–1,800 words
- CTA: Book a free 15-minute discovery call
- Outline:
  1. The problem with conventional "treat the symptom" medicine
  2. What integrative medicine actually means
  3. The evidence behind it (cite NICE, BMJ studies)
  4. What a first appointment at Insight Clinic looks like
  5. Who it's best suited for (chronic conditions, fatigue, digestive issues, mental health)
  6. FAQ: "Is integrative medicine the same as alternative medicine?"

**GMB Posts (3x this week):**
1. "New to integrative health? Here's what to expect at your first Insight Clinic appointment." [Link to blog]
2. "We're based at Chapel Market, Islington — 2 minutes from Angel tube. Meet our team →" [Team photo]
3. "Quick health tip: Walking 20 minutes a day reduces systemic inflammation by up to 12%. Our practitioners can show you what else works. Book →"

**Instagram (4 posts):**
1. Clinic exterior/interior welcome post with location tag
2. Practitioner spotlight: headshot + one sentence on their approach
3. Educational carousel: "5 signs your body is asking for integrative support"
4. Patient journey infographic: "What happens at your first appointment"
