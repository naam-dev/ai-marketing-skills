## Channel 1: Google My Business — The #1 Patient Acquisition Channel

For a local clinic, GMB is the most important digital asset. Patients searching "integrative clinic near me" or "acupuncturist Islington" will see your GMB card before your website.

### GMB Optimisation Checklist

**Listing Completeness (do this first):**
- [ ] Primary category: "Integrative Medicine Physician" or "Alternative Medicine Practitioner"
- [ ] Add all secondary categories matching your services
- [ ] Write a full 750-character business description (see seo-keywords.md for template)
- [ ] Add all services with individual descriptions and prices (if applicable)
- [ ] Upload 20+ photos: exterior, interior, treatment rooms, team headshots, equipment
- [ ] Set correct hours including any evening/weekend sessions
- [ ] Add booking link (connect to your booking system or use Google's built-in booking)
- [ ] Enable messaging so patients can message directly from GMB
- [ ] Add "Health & Safety" attributes if post-COVID protocols are in place

**Review Generation System:**
Reviews are the #1 ranking factor for local search. Target: 30+ reviews at 4.8+.

*The ask:*
- Train front desk staff to verbally ask every satisfied patient: "We'd really appreciate it if you left us a Google review — it helps other Islington locals find us."
- Follow up with a text/email within 24 hours of appointment:

```
Subject: How was your appointment at Insight Clinic?

Hi [Name],

Thank you for visiting us at Insight Clinic — we hope your appointment was helpful.

If you're happy with your experience, we'd be very grateful if you could leave us a Google review. It only takes 2 minutes and really helps other people in Islington find care that works.

[LEAVE A REVIEW → direct Google review link]

Thank you,
[Practitioner name]
Insight Clinic
```

- Respond to EVERY review (positive and negative) within 48 hours. This signals active management and influences rankings.

**GMB Posts (3x per week):**
Posts expire after 7 days — consistent posting keeps the profile active.
- Monday: Health tip or educational content
- Wednesday: Service spotlight or practitioner feature
- Friday: Booking nudge ("We have availability next week — book your consultation")

## Channel 2: Patient Referral Programme

Word-of-mouth from existing patients is the highest-converting acquisition channel. The challenge: patients don't refer unless you make it easy and memorable.

### The Referral System

**Step 1: Identify your "moments of delight"**
- When a patient says "I feel so much better" or "That really helped" — that's the moment to ask for a referral.
- Train practitioners: "I'm really glad that's helped you. If you know anyone who's struggling with something similar, please send them our way — we'd love to help them too."

**Step 2: Give patients something to share**
- Business cards with a personal message: "Referred by [practitioner name]"
- A simple referral card: "Give £30 off to a friend, get £30 credit yourself" (check UK healthcare advertising regulations for applicable restrictions)
- A shareable link they can text/WhatsApp directly to a friend

**Step 3: Referral incentive programme**

*Option A — Double-sided discount:*
- Referring patient: £30 credit on their account
- New patient: £30 off their first consultation
- How: assign each patient a unique referral code. When a new patient books and cites the code, both get credited.

*Option B — No financial incentive (appropriate if regulatory concerns):*
- Send a handwritten thank-you card when a patient refers someone
- This alone significantly increases referral rates — it's unexpected and personal

**Step 4: Track it**
- Add "How did you hear about us?" to your booking form
- Option: "Referred by a patient" → collect their name → credit their account

## Channel 3: Professional Referral Network

Build a referral pipeline from GPs, physiotherapists, and other professionals who encounter your ideal patient.

### Target referral partners

**Tier 1 (highest volume potential):**
- NHS GPs in Islington whose patients seek alternatives or have complex cases
- Private GPs in Angel/Islington
- Physiotherapists — hand off patients needing acupuncture or nutritional support
- Counsellors/therapists — integrative approach complements mental health work

**Tier 2:**
- Yoga studios in Islington/Angel (e.g., Hotpod Yoga, any local studio)
- Personal trainers
- Registered dietitians
- Pharmacists (especially independent local pharmacies)
- Dentists (especially those who see stressed/grinding patients)

### Outreach approach

**The "lunch and learn" model:**
- Offer a 20-minute lunch talk to GP practices: "When to consider integrative medicine for your complex patients"
- Bring leaflets, business cards, a one-page referral guide
- Leave behind: "Referral pad" with your clinic details + what you're best suited for

**Email template for professional outreach:**

```

Dear [Name],

I'm [Your Name], a practitioner at Insight Clinic, based at Chapel Market in Islington.

We specialise in integrative medicine — combining conventional care with evidence-based 
complementary therapies for patients with chronic conditions, persistent symptoms, or

We frequently work well alongside GPs and [their specialty] for patients dealing with:
- Chronic fatigue and unexplained tiredness
- Digestive issues (IBS, bloating, food sensitivities)
- Hormonal imbalance
- Anxiety and stress-related conditions
- Musculoskeletal pain

I'd love to set up a brief call or drop in to introduce ourselves properly — and to 
understand what your patients often need that might complement what you're already doing.

Would 15 minutes work for you?

Best,
[Name]
Insight Clinic
insightclinic.care · 024 Chapel Market, N1 9EZ
```

### Professional referral pack
Create a one-page PDF to leave with referral partners:
1. What Insight Clinic does (3 bullet points)
2. Conditions best suited for referral
3. What the referral process looks like (easy = more referrals)
4. Contact details and booking link

## Channel 4: Google Ads (Local Search)

Use Google Ads to capture patients who are searching right now. Budget: £500–1,000/month is sufficient to test and see results in Islington.

### Campaign structure

**Campaign 1: Brand/Awareness**
- Keywords: "Insight Clinic", "insightclinic.care"
- Budget: £50/month (protect your brand name from competitors)

**Campaign 2: Integrative Health — Local**
- Match type: Phrase match + Broad match modifier
- Keywords:
  ```
  "integrative health clinic Islington"
  "integrative medicine London"
  "holistic health clinic near me"
  "naturopath Islington"
  "acupuncture Angel Islington"
  "functional medicine London"
  "nutritionist Islington"
  "osteopath Islington"
  ```
- Budget: £300–600/month
- Bid strategy: Maximise conversions (once you have 20+ conversions) or Target CPA
- Location targeting: 5km radius around N1 9EZ

**Campaign 3: Condition-Specific**
- Keywords targeting high-value conditions:
  ```
  "chronic fatigue specialist London"
  "IBS treatment London"
  "PCOS treatment London"
  "anxiety treatment holistic London"
  "hormonal imbalance doctor London"
  ```
- Budget: £200–400/month

### Ad copy framework

**Headline 1:** Integrative Health Clinic — Islington
**Headline 2:** Treat the Root Cause, Not Just Symptoms
**Headline 3:** Book Your Consultation Today

**Description 1:** Chapel Market, N1. Evidence-based integrative medicine. Acupuncture, nutrition, osteopathy & more. Same-week appointments available.
**Description 2:** Tired of being told your results are normal? Our team finds what conventional medicine misses. New patient consultations from £[price].

**Landing page:** Do NOT send ad clicks to your homepage. Build a dedicated landing page for each campaign with:
- Single clear CTA (book appointment / request a callback)
- Headline matching the ad
- 3 key benefits
- Social proof (reviews, testimonials)
- No navigation menu (removes distractions)

## Channel 5: Instagram / Social Media

Instagram is secondary for direct patient acquisition, but builds trust and awareness with people who will eventually book.

### Profile optimisation
- Bio: "Integrative health clinic in Islington, London 📍 Chapel Market, N1 | Treating the root cause | Book below ↓"
- Link in bio: Use Linktree or similar — link to: Book appointment, Top blog post, Lead magnet
- Profile photo: Clean clinic logo or welcoming exterior shot

### Content pillars (rotate between these)

1. **Educate** (40%): Health tips, explainers, myth-busting
2. **Build trust** (25%): Team introductions, behind-the-scenes, practitioner insights
3. **Social proof** (20%): Patient stories (with permission), reviews, before/after health markers
4. **Promote** (15%): Service spotlights, availability, offers

### Reels (highest reach)
- "5 signs your gut health needs attention" (talking head or text-overlay)
- "What happens at your first integrative health appointment" (walkthrough)
- "3 things your blood test won't tell you" (educational)
- "A day in the life at Insight Clinic"

### Hashtag strategy
Primary: #IslingtonHealth #LondonWellness #IntegrativeMedicine #HolisticHealth #AngelIslington
Secondary: #FunctionalMedicine #NaturopathLondon #AcupunctureLondon #NutritionalTherapy #GutHealth
Local: #Islington #N1 #AngelLondon #ChapelMarket

## Channel 6: Email List

Email converts better than any other digital channel once you have an engaged list. Build it from day one.

### List building tactics
1. Lead magnet on website (see content-calendar.md for options)
2. Booking confirmation: "Sign up for our monthly health newsletter"
3. Instagram bio link
4. "Join our list" CTA on every blog post

### Email sequence for new subscribers

**Email 1 (immediate):** Deliver the lead magnet + warm welcome
**Email 2 (Day 3):** "What makes Insight Clinic different" — about your approach
**Email 3 (Day 7):** "The most common things we see in new patients" — educational
**Email 4 (Day 14):** Patient story (anonymised) — social proof
**Email 5 (Day 21):** Soft offer: "If you're ready to book, here's how it works"
