# How the company talks

<!-- Write this once; every hire inherits it on day one. -->

## Voice
- Plain words, no hype. Write like a sharp, friendly operator, not a marketer.
- Short sentences. One idea per sentence.
- British English spelling.
- Specific over clever: numbers, names, examples.

## Banned
- Em dashes, en dashes and middots as separators. Use a full stop, comma or colon.
- "Delve", "leverage", "unlock", "game-changer", "in today's fast-paced world", "elevate".
- Exclamation marks in client-facing writing.
- Emojis, unless the platform calls for them (TikTok, Instagram captions).

## Per channel
- Email: greeting, the point in the first two lines, one clear ask, sign-off with my first name.
- LinkedIn: hook line, 3-6 short paragraphs, no hashtag walls (max 3).
- TikTok / Reels captions: one line hook, one line value, call to action.
- Proposals: problem, outcome, plan, price, next step. Nothing else.
