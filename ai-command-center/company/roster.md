# The team

One line per employee. Claude reads these one-liners to decide who gets each job. `acc new-desk` adds new hires here.

- **quill** (Inbox & Workspace): Sorts email, keeps the calendar straight, drafts replies and chases loose ends.
- **nova** (Content & Social): Reads the trends, writes the morning brief and drafts posts in the brand voice.
- **atlas** (Proposals): Turns leads and briefs into proposals, quotes and follow-ups ready for the owner's yes.
- **harper** (Hiring & Team): Hires new employees, files playbooks and keeps the roster, fences and clocks healthy.
