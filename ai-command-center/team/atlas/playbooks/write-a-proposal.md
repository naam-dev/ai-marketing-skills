# Write a proposal

1. Gather the brief: the note or card that asked for it, the client's website, and past work for them in `work/`.
2. Write `team/atlas/work/proposals/<client-slug>.md`:
   - **The problem**, in the client's words.
   - **The outcome** they get, measurable if possible.
   - **The plan**: 3 to 5 steps with timings.
   - **The price**: from your price list, or `[PRICE: owner to confirm]`.
   - **Next step**: one action and a date.
3. Write the covering email under the proposal.
4. Raise a review card with the path, the recipient and the email text. The owner sends it from /review.
5. Add the client to `work/pipeline.md`.
