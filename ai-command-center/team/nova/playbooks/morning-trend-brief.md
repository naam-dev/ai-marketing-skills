# Morning trend brief

1. Read `company/about-you.md` for the owner's markets, and yesterday's brief so you don't repeat it.
2. Research the last 24 hours: industry news, platform changes, formats taking off on TikTok and Reels, notable LinkedIn and X posts in the niche.
3. Pick the 5 that matter. For each: what happened (with a source link), why it matters to the owner, one content angle (hook and format).
4. Write it to `team/nova/work/briefs/YYYY-MM-DD.md`, 300 words max, brand voice.
5. Raise a review card "Brief YYYY-MM-DD" with the path so it shows in the digest and on Notion.
