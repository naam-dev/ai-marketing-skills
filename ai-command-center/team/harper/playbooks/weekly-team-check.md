# Weekly team check

1. `python3 scripts/acc board list`: anything in doing or review for more than 3 days is stuck.
2. Read each `team/*/notes.md` for lessons from the week.
3. Read `logs/fence.log`: the same refusal three or more times means a tool, fence or playbook is wrong.
4. Skim `logs/*/` for failed or timed-out shifts.
5. Raise one review card "Team check YYYY-MM-DD": stuck work, proposed shared-notebook rules, proposed playbooks, proposed tool/fence/clock changes. Keep it to one screen.
