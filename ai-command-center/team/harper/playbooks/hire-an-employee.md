# Propose a new hire

1. Write down the three answers (from the request or your weekly check):
   1. **The job**: one sentence, and what "done well" looks like.
   2. **The clock**: when they should work on their own.
   3. **The fence**: what they read or draft; what waits for a yes.
2. Check the roster. If an existing employee could do it with a new playbook, propose that instead.
3. Suggest a short lowercase name that isn't taken.
4. Raise a review card "New hire: <name>" with the three answers and the exact `acc new-desk` command. The owner runs /hire to build it.
